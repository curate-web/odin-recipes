Spanish Omelette

Description

This Spanish omelette features fried potatoes, onions, and eggs for a filling meal. Home cooks praise its adaptability for adding cheese, bacon, or extra veggies.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup olive oil
  • 8 ounces potatoes, thinly sliced
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 medium tomatoes, peeled, seeded, and coarsely chopped (Optional)
  • 2 green onions, chopped (Optional)

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add potatoes; season lightly with salt and black pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and crisp, 10 to 14 minutes. Add onions; cook and stir until soft and beginning to brown, 6 to 8 minutes.
  3. Whisk eggs in a bowl; season with salt and black pepper.
  4. Add eggs to the skillet; stir gently to combine with potatoes and onion. Reduce the heat to low; cook until eggs begin to brown on the bottom, 4 to 5 minutes.
  5. Loosen omelette with a spatula. Place a large plate over the skillet and carefully invert to release omelette onto the plate. Slide omelette, uncooked-side down, back into the skillet; cook until eggs are set, 4 to 5 minutes.
  6. Garnish with tomato and green onion. Serve warm.
